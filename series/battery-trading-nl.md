<h1>Trading a battery in the Dutch electricity markets</h1>
<p>
  A series on how a grid-scale battery in the Netherlands can earn money, built up one market at a time.
  Every part uses the same example, a 1 MW / 2 MWh battery and 2025 Dutch market data, so the results
  can be compared and added up. Each part answers one question and ends with what it assumes.
</p>
<p>
  The order follows the markets: first a map of who does what, then day-ahead, imbalance, frequency reserves
  and intraday on their own, then all of them together. The last parts replace hindsight with forecasts.
</p>
